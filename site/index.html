The long way home
I took the long way home today. Nothing dramatic, just the turn by the park instead of the station. The daylilies along the fence were still open even though the light was going.
Called Sam on the walk. We talked about almost nothing, her noisy neighbour, a book neither of us has finished.
I think I needed that more than advice.
…and how did that feel?
